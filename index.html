The Method

Five links in one chain of reasoning

Every argument submitted here is walked through the same sūtra — the same thread — that Nyāya logicians used to test a claim before it could stand in debate. Break the chain at any link, and the argument fails with it.

१

Pratijñā

Claim or Proposition — the assertion being put forward for debate.

२

Hetu

Reason — the ground offered in support of the claim.

३

Udāharaṇa

Example — a familiar instance that illustrates the reasoning.

४

Upanaya

Application — connecting the example back to the present case.

५

Nigamana

Conclusion — the claim, now earned rather than merely asserted.
